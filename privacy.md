# Privacy Policy

*Strybe - Big Lemon LLC. Effective 2026-10-27.*

## The short version

Strybe has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device. Big Lemon LLC ("we", "us") never receives it and cannot see it.

## What Strybe stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following in its own storage on your phone:

- The name you give it.
- Your habits, weekly goals, check-ins, streaks and recaps.
- A profile photo, if you choose one - a copy of the picture you pick.
- Your reminder settings and app preferences.

None of this is sent anywhere. We have no copy of it.

## Permissions

- Notifications - reminders are scheduled on your device by the app itself. There is no push server, and turning them off changes nothing else.
- Camera and photo library - used only when you choose a profile photo, and only the photo you pick is kept.

You can change these at any time in your device's settings.

## What we collect

Nothing. Strybe contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools of our own. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we never have it.

## Apple, Google and device backups

- The App Store and Google Play handle downloads and any purchases under their own privacy policies.
- If you have opted in through your device's settings, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with developers. That is their service, under their terms, and we cannot identify you from it.
- If your device backs up to iCloud or Google, those backups can include Strybe's data, under the provider's own policy.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. To erase everything, use Settings > Reset Local Data in the app, or delete the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing on our side to delete.

## Children

Strybe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone - including children.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since all of it lives on your device, you can exercise those rights directly in the app. You are still welcome to contact us with any question.

## Changes, and features we may add

We may one day offer optional accounts or cloud sync, analytics or crash reporting, or paid features. Any of these could mean some data leaving your device. Before that happens we will update this policy, change its effective date, and tell you in the app - and anything that sends your data off the device will ask first.
